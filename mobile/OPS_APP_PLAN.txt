DELIVERY FAST COURIER OPERATIONS APP - PLAN
===========================================

Android app (Flutter) for operations staff, next to the merchant and rider apps (mobile/).
Status: PHASE 1 BUILT (27 Oct 2026) - mobile/ops_app, package bd.com.deliveryfast.operations,
  "Delivery Fast Operations" 1.0.0, with push notifications. APK on the Courier Operations sidebar
  ("Get the operations app", /downloads/delivery-fast-operations.apk).
Written 26 Oct 2026; updated 26 Oct 2026 with the owner's decisions:
  - hub operators and operations managers both get the app from Phase 1;
  - the hubs' internet is reliable (no offline mode needed);
  - the live map and cash transfers to the central hub are in Phase 1.

WHO USES IT
 - Hub operators (hub managers / desk staff): the hub floor - scanning parcels in and out,
   handing parcels to riders, taking riders' cash, the hub pickup counter.
 - Operations managers: watching the day, assigning riders, sorting out problems, on the move.
 Each person sees only their own hubs (as in the web panel). Franchise hubs can be added later.

WHY AN APP (the web panel stays for desk work)
 - Fast scanning on the floor: phone camera, AND handheld scanners (Zebra / Honeywell /
   Urovo and similar) that type the barcode like a keyboard. One parcel per second.
 - Beep + vibration + a clear message per scan, then ready for the next parcel
   (same scanner as the rider app).
 - Push notifications for new work (new orders, pickup requests, problems, cash waiting).
 - Managers can follow the day and act from their phone.

WHAT STAYS ON THE WEB PANEL
 Reports and analytics downloads, Excel imports / bulk updates, Accounts (settlements, bills,
 rider pay, franchise money), CN stock, merchant administration, settings.

Shared code: mobile/df_core (sign-in, API, scanner, push, support, barcode).


PHASE 1 - THE HUB FLOOR, THE DAY, THE MAP AND THE CASH  [about 8-10 days]
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
For hub operators AND operations managers from day one. Almost no new backend work: every
action below already has an API endpoint (hub desk, hub receive, assign rider, linehaul bags /
trips, rider and vehicle locations, cash slips, cash transfers).

 1. Sign in (operations manager, hub operator), choose the hub when the person has several.
    Push notifications on.

 2. Today: the hub's numbers at a glance - new orders, pickups to assign, at the hub,
    out for delivery, delivered, failed, problems, SLA late-risk, riders on shift.
    Each number opens its list.

 3. SCAN DESK (the heart of the app) - choose the task, set it up once, then scan
    parcel after parcel (camera or handheld scanner), beep + result + running list:
      - Receive entry: parcels arriving at the hub (from pickup riders, drop-offs,
        transfers, failed deliveries coming back).
      - Out for delivery: choose the rider, scan the parcels they take.
      - Transfer out: scan parcels into a bag / onto a trip to another hub
        (manifest MF-); Transfer receive: scan what arrived, see what is missing.
      - Delivery entry / Failed delivery entry: for riders who report at the desk.
      - Return rider assign / Return entry: parcels going back to merchants.
      - Collect from hub (Hub Pickup counter): scan, check the collection code, hand over,
        take the COD.
      - Pickup entry: parcels a merchant brings to the hub.

 4. Assign pickup riders: new orders and pickup requests waiting -> choose a rider
    (by zone, how busy they are). Assign delivery riders for parcels at the hub.

 5. Rider cash: scan the rider's handover slip (HS-), count the cash, confirm
    (short / extra recorded). List of riders still holding cash.

 6. Find a parcel: scan or type -> full details, tracking timeline, who has it now,
    call the recipient / merchant / rider, the actions allowed for its status.

 7. Problems: exceptions, failed deliveries, needs info - see and act (try again,
    return to merchant, contact).

 8. Live map: riders on shift and vehicles on the road, on a map (positions the rider app
    already sends); tap a rider to see their load and call them, tap a vehicle to see its
    trip and expected arrival.

 9. Cash to the central hub (HT-): the hub's cash on hand, send a hub transfer to the
    central hub, follow it until it is received; at the central hub, receive transfers
    and record short / extra.

10. Support: requests from the app (same as the other apps).

Internet at the hubs is reliable, so actions are sent at once. A scan that fails to send
(server busy, brief drop) is retried automatically with its Idempotency-Key, so it is never
applied twice; no offline mode.


PHASE 2 - RIDERS AND THE NETWORK  [about 4-5 days]
--------------------------------------------------
 1. Riders: who is on shift / on break / late, attendance, today's load per rider,
    call a rider, reassign their parcels.
 2. Transfers on the road: trips between hubs, expected arrivals, discrepancies.
 3. SLA late-risk board with quick reassignment.
 4. End of day for the hub: what is still at the hub, cash on hand, close the day.
 5. Printing: labels and manifests on a Bluetooth thermal printer.
 6. Bangla language.


PHASE 3 - OPTIONAL  [about 3-4 days]
------------------------------------
 1. Reports on the phone (read only): rider report, hub transfer report, daily summary.
 2. Approvals that operations make (e.g. merchant address change requests).
 3. Franchise hub version (franchise staff see only their hub).


TECHNICAL
---------
 - New app: mobile/ops_app (package bd.com.deliveryfast.operations), signed with the same
   Delivery Fast key, APK download link in Courier Operations' sidebar.
 - Roles: OPS_MANAGER and HUB_OPERATOR (custom roles based on them too); the hubs a
   person can see come from the API, as in the web panel.
 - Handheld scanners: the scan screen also listens for keyboard-wedge input (scanner
   types the code + Enter), so a hardware scanner and the camera both work.
 - Push: the in-app notifications staff already get (new work, problems, cash) also
   come as pushes (the push system built for the merchant and rider apps).
 - Built for phones and the emulator (ARM + x86_64), tested with screen tests on saved
   API responses plus a real run on the emulator.
