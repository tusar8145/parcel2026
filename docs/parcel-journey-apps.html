Parcel journey: who taps what

Banking & Financial merchants (documents, no cash to collect). The journey is cut into sections; each section says which app, where to tap, step by step. Names in boxes are exactly as on the screen (Rider app 1.7.2, Operations app 1.8.0).

The sections in order

  1. Pickup: rider assigned → handed in at the hub · without the rider app: 1B
  2. Hub to hub transfer · Operations app only, no rider
  3. Delivery: rider assigned at the hub → delivered, or not delivered and back at the hub · without the rider app: 3B
  4. Return: to the central hub (hub to hub for returns) · Operations app only
  5. Return: rider assigned → returned to the merchant · without the rider app: 5B
  6. Grouping the actions by the work · rider app and Operations app groups, and what isn't needed

Rider app bottom tabs

Today · Pickups · Scan · Deliveries · Account

Scan tab: Pickup from merchant · Hand over at hub · Receive from hub (optional) · Start deliveries · Deliver · Could not deliver · Return to merchant

Operations app bottom tabs

Today · Scan · Map · Cash · More

Scan tab groups: RECEIVE AT HUB · DELIVERY · RIDER BACK AT THE HUB · HUB TO HUB · RETURNS TO MERCHANT · CN LABELS

Scanning (both apps)

The camera opens and stays on: scan parcel after parcel. A handheld scanner works too. No barcode? Tap Type it instead. Each scan beeps and shows its result. Press back when done.

Operations app: the hub you work at is asked once (Which hub are you working at?); change it in More › Change hub.

1

Pickup: rider assigned → handed in at the hub

From the merchant's booking until the documents are received at the hub.

Merchant appOperations appRider app
  1. Merchant books · merchant's office
    Today›Book a consignment›fill in the recipient›Book consignment
    Status Pending operations confirmation
  2. Assign the pickup rider · anywhere
    More›Assign pickup riders
    • Tick the parcels (or All at the top).
    • Tap Assign … at the bottom, choose the rider in Who picks them up?.
    Status Pickup assigned · the rider gets a notification.
  3. Rider sees the pickup · anywhere
    Pickups (tab)
    • One card per merchant: name, address, N parcel(s) to collect, buttons Call, Map, Collect.
    • The rider goes to the merchant's address.
  4. Collect at the merchant · merchant's office
    Pickups›merchant card›Collect›Scan parcels
    or Scan›Pickup from merchant›Which pickup? choose the merchant
    • Scan each document the merchant hands over. The screen counts Expected · Done · Missing.
    • No label to scan: tap ⋮ on the parcel › Collected (no scan). Problem: ⋮ › Report a problem.
    Status Picked up · now under Pickups › Carrying.
  5. Hand over at the hub · hub
    Scan›Hand over at hub
    or Pickups›Carrying card›Hand over at hub›Scan to hand over
    • Scan each parcel as it is given to the hub. A mistake can be undone (Undo) until the hub scans it.
    Shows handed over, waiting for the hub to scan them in.
  6. Receive at the hub · hub
    Scan›RECEIVE AT HUB›Receive at hub
    • Scan every parcel the rider handed in.
    Status At hub · the documents are now in the hub's stock.

Next: delivered from this hub → section 3; another hub delivers → section 2.

1B

Pickup when the rider has no app

The rider collects on paper; the hub records it when the rider is back.

Operations app
  1. Assign the pickup rider · anywhere
    More›Assign pickup riders›tick the parcels›Assign …›Who picks them up?
    Give the rider the list (or the labels). Status Pickup assigned
  2. Rider is back with the documents · hub
    Scan›RECEIVE AT HUB›Pickup entry
    • Scan each parcel: it is picked up for that rider and received at this hub in the same scan.
    Status At hub

Next: 3B (or section 2 if another hub delivers).

2

Hub to hub transfer

The pickup hub doesn't serve the recipient's area. No rider in this part: hub A sends, hub B receives.

Operations app (hub A)Operations app (hub B)
  1. Hub A sends the parcels · hub A
    Scan›HUB TO HUB›Transfer out
    • Send to which hub? → The hub serving each parcel's delivery area (automatic, usual choice) or a hub by name.
    • Scan each parcel as it is packed for the vehicle.
    Status In transit · hub B sees them on its way.
  2. Hub B receives them · hub B
    Scan›RECEIVE AT HUB›Receive at hub
    • Scan each parcel that arrived. (A bag number BG-… receives the whole bag.)
    Status At hub (hub B)
Shortcut at hub B: Scan › Receive & assign rider receives a parcel still in transit and gives it to the rider in one scan (section 3, step 1).

Next: section 3 at hub B.

3

Delivery: rider assigned at the hub → delivered, or not delivered and back at the hub

The rider uses the rider app.

Operations appRider app
  1. Give the parcels to the delivery rider · hub
    Scan›DELIVERY›Receive & assign rider›Who takes them? choose the rider
    • Scan each parcel the rider takes.
    or from a list: More›Assign delivery riders›tick›Assign …›Who delivers them?
    Status Assigned · they appear in the rider's Deliveries.
  2. Rider checks the parcels (optional) · hub
    Scan›Receive from hub (optional)
    • Each scan says whether the parcel is assigned to this rider.
  3. Leave for delivery · hub
    Scan›Start deliveries
    or one by one: Deliveries›parcel›Start delivery
    • Scan each parcel while loading. The recipient gets the delivery code by SMS.
    Status Out for delivery
  4. At the recipient: delivered · recipient's address
    Scan›Deliver›scan the parcel
    or Deliveries›parcel›Delivered
    • Received by: the name, and who they are (Self, or a relation).
    • Proof: Recipient's code (from the SMS), Signature, or Photo (Take a photo of the signed invoice), as the parcel requires.
    • Confirm delivery.
    Status Delivered · the merchant sees it at once.
  5. At the recipient: could not deliver · recipient's address
    Deliveries›parcel›Could not deliver
    • Why? choose the reason.
    • What next? Try again (deliver tomorrow) or Return to sender.
    • Save. The card now says to hand it in at the hub.
    Several at once: Scan›Could not deliver›reason + Try again / Return to sender›Start scanning
    Status Retry scheduled (still with the rider)
  6. End of the day: the rider hands in what wasn't delivered · hub
    Scan›RIDER BACK AT THE HUB›Receive from rider
    • Which rider is handing in? choose the rider.
    • Failed deliveries: what next? As the rider chose (deliver again / return) (usual), or Deliver again, or Return to the sender.
    • Scan each parcel. The line on top counts what is still with the rider.
    Status At hub. Try again parcels go out again tomorrow (back to step 1). Return to sender parcels are on the returns list → section 4.
3B

Delivery when the rider has no app

The hub does every step in the Operations app; the rider brings back signed slips.

Operations app
  1. Rider leaves with the parcels · hub
    Scan›DELIVERY›Out for delivery›Who is going out? choose the rider
    • Scan each parcel as the rider loads it. Parcels still at the hub are given to this rider and sent out in the same scan. The recipient gets the delivery code by SMS.
    Status Out for delivery
  2. Rider is back: the delivered ones · hub
    Scan›RIDER BACK AT THE HUB›Delivery entry
    • For each signed slip: Received by (optional), Relation to the recipient, Note (optional), Photo of the slip.
    • Tap Scan the slip and scan it. The form clears for the next slip.
    Status Delivered (for that rider)
  3. Rider is back: the ones not delivered · hub
    Scan›RIDER BACK AT THE HUB›Failed delivery entry
    • Why could they not be delivered? choose the reason.
    • Try again (stays with the rider) or Back at the hub (received here now).
    • Start scanning, scan each parcel with that reason. Another reason: go back and start again.
    Back at the hub: status At hub. Try again: Retry scheduled; when the rider hands it in later, use Receive from rider (section 3, step 6).
A parcel that should go back to the merchant after a failed delivery: Receive from rider with Return to the sender, or section 4, step 1.
4

Return: to the central hub (hub to hub for returns)

Returns are put on runs at the central hub. A parcel going back that is at any other hub travels there first. Operations app only, no rider.

Operations app (branch hub)Operations app (central hub)
  1. Mark the parcel for return (only if nobody did yet) · branch hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›1 · Return issue
    • Why does it go back? choose the reason.
    • Rider (optional): leave No rider.
    • Send to … now (the central hub) switched on: the parcel also leaves for the central hub in the same scan (step 2 done).
    • Start scanning, scan each parcel.
    Not needed for parcels the rider chose Return to sender for, or the merchant asked to return: they are already on the list.
  2. Send the returns to the central hub · branch hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›2 · Return transfer
    • Scan each return as it leaves. The line on top counts the returns still held here.
    Status In transit to the central hub.
  3. Central hub receives them · central hub
    Scan›RECEIVE AT HUB›Receive at hub
    Status At hub (central) · still marked for return.
What's waiting: the yellow line under RETURNS TO MERCHANT (N held here · next: …), or 4 · Send out return runs for the list.

Next: section 5 at the central hub.

5

Return: rider assigned → returned to the merchant

At the central hub (or at a hub that returns directly to a nearby merchant). The rider uses the rider app.

Operations appRider app
  1. Give the returns to a rider · central hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›3 · Return rider assign
    • Who takes the returns? choose the rider.
    • Reason (for parcels without one) choose a reason (used only if a parcel has none).
    • Scan each parcel. Each merchant gets one return run (RR-…) for this rider.
    Status Return scheduled · the rider gets a notification.
    Returning straight from a branch hub (merchant nearby): 1 · Return issue with a rider chosen in Rider (optional) does this step there, without the central hub.
  2. Send the run out · central hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›4 · Send out return runs›the run›Send out›Send out
    The run is out with the rider. (If skipped, the run goes out by itself with the rider's first return.)
  3. Rider sees the returns · anywhere
    Today›N return run(s) to merchants
    or Account›Returns to merchants
    • Each run: merchant, address, parcels, Call.
  4. Hand them back to the merchant · merchant's office
    Scan›Return to merchant›Which return run? (only if several)
    or Account›Returns to merchants›run›Return›Who is taking the parcels back?›Start scanning
    • Scan each parcel the merchant takes. The line on top shows N of M returned; the run completes with the last one.
    Status Returned · the merchant confirms receipt on the merchant panel (Returns › Confirm received).
5B

Return when the rider has no app

The hub assigns and sends out; the rider brings back the run sheet signed by the merchant.

Operations app
  1. Give the returns to the rider · central hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›3 · Return rider assign›Who takes the returns?›reason›scan
    Status Return scheduled
  2. Rider leaves · central hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›4 · Send out return runs›Send out
    Print or write the run (RR-…) with its parcels for the merchant to sign.
  3. Rider is back with the signed sheet · hub
    Scan›RETURNS TO MERCHANT›5 · Return entry
    • Who took them back? (optional) type the name, Start scanning.
    • Scan each parcel the merchant took. The run completes with its last parcel.
    Status Returned
  4. A parcel the merchant didn't take · hub
    Scan›RIDER BACK AT THE HUB›Receive from rider
    Back At hub; assign it again with 3 · Return rider assign.
6

Grouping the actions by the work

A proposal: the actions of each app sorted into groups that follow the sections above, so a rider or hub operator finds the next step in the order the work happens. The last groups hold what is needed only sometimes, and what Banking & Financial merchants don't need. (The apps aren't changed by this page.)

Rider app 6 groups + 2

R1
Pickup at the merchantSection 1 · merchant's office
  • Pickups list (Call, Map)
  • Pickup from merchant / Collect › Scan parcels
  • Collected (no scan)
  • Report a problem (merchant closed, not ready)
R2
Hand in at the hubSection 1 · hub
  • Hand over at hub (Scan, or Pickups › Carrying)
  • Undo (before the hub scans it)
R3
Leave for deliverySection 3 · hub
  • Deliveries list (route, Directions)
  • Receive from hub (optional)
  • Start deliveries / Start delivery
R4
At the recipient's doorSection 3 · recipient's address
  • Deliver / Delivered › Received by, Proof, Confirm delivery
  • Could not deliver › Try again / Return to sender
  • Call the recipient, Send the code again
R5
Return to the merchantSection 5 · merchant's office
  • Returns to merchants (the runs; today shows N return run(s) to merchants)
  • Return to merchant › Return › scan
R6
My day & accountAny time
  • Start shift / location sharing
  • Saved actions (work saved while offline)
  • Help & support, Report a problem to support
  • Change password, Sign out

Only sometimesNot part of the daily steps
  • CN Merchant, CN_Scan: only when the merchant uses pre-printed CN labels
  • Receive from hub (optional) can be skipped: the hub's scan already gives the parcels to the rider
