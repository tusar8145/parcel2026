A little more clarity

Good to know.

Quick answers to common questions about sending and tracking your parcel.

Need more help? Talk to us
How do I track a parcel?

Enter the tracking ID from your booking or confirmation message in the tracking box above. You'll see the latest sample status and delivery milestones.

Can I send a parcel without signing up?

Yes. Use “Request a pickup” to submit a one-time domestic parcel, document, or ecommerce shipment request. The demo will generate a tracking reference for you.

How does cash on delivery work?

For eligible ecommerce shipments, a rider collects the order amount from the receiver. Your final merchant settlement and COD terms depend on your merchant plan.

Do franchise partners run local operations?

Yes. Franchise partners operate an agreed territory, with their own local hub and team coordinating merchant support, parcel handling, and rider operations.
